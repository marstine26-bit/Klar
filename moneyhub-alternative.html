FAQ

Common questions

Which banks are supported?

UK users can connect 2,000+ banks via Salt Edge open banking (Pro plan). SA users can manually import transactions. Direct bank connections for SA are on our roadmap.

What's included in the free plan?

The free plan includes transaction tracking, CSV import, and spending breakdowns — no credit card required. Net worth tracker, AI advisor, savings goals, and unlimited accounts unlock with Essential (£3.99/mo).

Does the AI advisor cost extra?

No. The AI advisor (powered by Groq/LLaMA) is included in the Essential plan and above at no extra charge.
