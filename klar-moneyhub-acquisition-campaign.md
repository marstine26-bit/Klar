# Klar × Moneyhub Closure: Acquisition Campaign Brief
*Prepared August 2026 — planning document only, nothing in here has been posted or sent*

---

## 1. The facts, verified (Aug 2026)

- Moneyhub's consumer app closes to existing users on **14 August 2026**. That date has now passed as of today (27 Aug 2026) — the shutdown is live, not upcoming. ([Wealthr](https://wealthr.co.uk/blog/moneyhub-closing-how-to-switch), [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/major-budgeting-app-used-thousands-064743138.html))
- **This is not a clean churn event.** Moneyhub struck a deal with **WPS Advisory** to continue running the app under new branding, "same platform underneath." Users were offered an opt-in from mid-July with a free premium trial, and could stay with minimal disruption. ([Moneyhub press release](https://moneyhub.com/press-releases/continuing-service-of-the-moneyhub-app/), [Moneyhub Help — Service Migration Q&A](https://moneyhubhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/32678340163217-Service-Migration-Q-A-s))
- Users who did **not** opt in or export/delete by **31 July 2026** have had accounts and data auto-deleted per GDPR. So the addressable pool is specifically people who *actively chose to leave* rather than accept the WPS migration — a self-selected, more engaged, more skeptical-of-the-incumbent segment. That's a better lead than a random Moneyhub user.
- Previous pricing was ~£1.49/mo or £14.99/yr — very cheap. Users are used to paying next to nothing for aggregation.
- **Competitors are already fishing this pond.** Emma has a live blog post explicitly targeting these users ("Best Moneyhub Alternative: Why Emma Is the Perfect Replacement"). Money To The Masses, Wealthr, Aureli, and others have published comparison/alternatives roundups since the announcement, recommending Snoop (cheapest, ~£3.33/mo annual), Emma (premium, £9.99+/mo), YNAB, WealthR, and PocketSmith/ActualBudget. **Klar does not currently appear in any of these roundups.** ([Money To The Masses](https://moneytothemasses.com/news/best-moneyhub-alternatives), [Wealthr](https://wealthr.co.uk/blog/best-moneyhub-alternatives-uk), [Aureli](https://aureli.app/blog/moneyhub-alternatives-uk))

**Implication:** the "window" framing in the competitive brief needs updating — this isn't a future opportunity, it's happening now and competitors have a head start on SEO/content for these exact search terms. Speed matters more than originally scoped.

## 2. Target audience

**Who they are:** UK consumers who paid (however little) for a dedicated aggregation/budgeting app, valued seeing all accounts in one Open-Banking-connected dashboard, and were engaged enough to notice the shutdown announcement and actively decide to leave rather than passively accept the WPS-branded continuation.

**What they used Moneyhub for:** net-worth / full-picture account aggregation across multiple banks, savings, and pensions; passive dashboard viewing rather than active budgeting rules (Moneyhub's UI was closer to "read-only mirror" than YNAB-style active budgeting); low price tolerance (£1.49/mo).

**What they will expect from a replacement, and where Klar falls short today:**
- Automatic multi-bank Open Banking sync → Klar has UK bank feeds via Salt Edge on paid tiers (per the landing page), but this is not the same breadth/maturity Moneyhub had. Be accurate about this, don't imply full parity.
- Near-zero price → Klar's Essential tier (£3.99/mo) is ~2.7x Moneyhub's old price. This needs addressing head-on, not glossed over.
- Passive net-worth view → Klar has this natively (net worth tracker on Essential+).

**Segment to prioritize:** the subset who searched "Moneyhub alternative" or "Moneyhub closing" in the last 6 weeks — i.e., people currently mid-decision, not people who already picked Emma or Snoop weeks ago. Late but not too late.

---

## 3. Positioning / message

**Core angle:** *"Moneyhub showed you the whole picture. Klar tells you what to do about it."*

Don't compete on aggregation breadth — Klar will lose that comparison against Emma or Snoop right now. Compete on what happens after the dashboard loads: an AI advisor that actually answers "am I on track" and "what should I cut," at a price still below Emma, with a free tier so switching costs nothing to try.

**Three supporting messages, in priority order:**
1. **"Free to look, no card required."** Removes the friction of trying yet another app after already being burned by one shutting down. Directly counters shutdown fatigue.
2. **"An AI advisor, not just another dashboard."** Moneyhub (per the competitive brief) had no AI. This is the one differentiator where Klar beats every recommended alternative except Cleo (which is US-focused/waitlisted for UK). Lead here, not on bank-feed breadth.
3. **Honest bridge on Open Banking:** *"Klar connects to UK banks via Salt Edge on paid plans. If you want to start today, manual import plus the AI advisor gets you a clearer answer in minutes than a dashboard alone ever gave you."* Never claim parity with Moneyhub's aggregation depth. Frame manual entry as a deliberate speed-to-insight trade-off, not an apology.

**What NOT to do:** don't run "we're just like Moneyhub" messaging — it invites a direct feature-for-feature comparison Klar loses. Don't bury the £3.99 price behind "free" language; the WPS-fatigued audience is price-sensitive and will bounce hard on a bait-and-switch feel.

## 4. Channels (solo-founder budget, ranked by effort/impact)

### 4a. "Moneyhub alternative" content page — highest priority, do first
A single dedicated landing page (not a blog post) targeting the exact search terms competitors already rank for: "Moneyhub alternative," "Moneyhub closing what to do," "Moneyhub shut down."
- Cost: your time only. No ad spend.
- Because competitors are already ranking, this needs backlinks/distribution (4b, 4c) to have a chance — pure SEO alone will lag Emma's head start by weeks.
- Outline in Section 5 below.

### 4b. Reddit / community angle
- r/UKPersonalFinance and r/FIREUK both had organic threads about the Moneyhub closure (visible in the search results ecosystem — Threads/X posts from Money To The Masses got engagement). A genuine, disclosed "I built a budgeting app, here's how it compares honestly including where it falls short" post — not a stealth plug — fits Reddit's tolerance for solo-founder transparency far better than a sales pitch.
- Must disclose founder status per subreddit self-promotion rules; lead with the honest gap (no live Open Banking parity) or it will be deleted/downvoted.
- Cost: time only. Highest-risk/highest-authenticity channel — one good thread historically outperforms paid ads for niche fintech tools.

### 4c. Direct outreach to the people already writing about it
Money To The Masses, Wealthr, Aureli, and yourmoney.lumio-app.com have all published "Moneyhub alternatives" roundups that do **not** currently list Klar. A short, honest email pitch to each ("here's what Klar does differently, here's what it doesn't do yet") asking to be considered for their existing/updated roundup is low-cost outreach with a very targeted, already-warm readership (these posts are actively being read by exactly the shutdown audience). This is not "sending on the user's behalf" — flagging it here as an idea; actually drafting/sending requires separate sign-off.

**Not recommended right now:** paid social/search ads. CPCs in UK fintech are high, the audience is a few thousand people at most, and a free organic post (4b) or earned placement (4c) will outperform paid spend at this budget size.

## 5. Landing/comparison page outline (honest, no overclaiming)

**URL suggestion:** `/moneyhub-alternative` as a dedicated page, distinct from the main landing page, so messaging can stay narrowly targeted without cluttering the investor-facing homepage.

1. **Headline:** "Moneyhub is closing. Here's what Klar does — and doesn't do — instead."
2. **One-line acknowledgment of the shutdown** with the actual date (14 Aug 2026) and a neutral note that WPS Advisory is continuing the service for those who opted in — so Klar isn't seen as misrepresenting the situation.
3. **Honest comparison table** (3 rows, not 20): Price, AI advisor, Bank connections. Show Klar's Salt Edge UK feeds as "paid plans, growing coverage" rather than implying full parity — do not claim more than the FAQ on the main landing page already claims.
4. **"What you'll need to do differently" box:** a short, unhedged paragraph that manual/CSV import is the free-tier starting point and live bank feeds are a paid-tier feature — set expectations before signup, not after.
5. **AI advisor demo strip** — reuse the existing `.ai-strip` component/copy from klarmoney-landing.html for visual and message consistency.
6. **CTA:** "Start free — no card needed" linking to `/app`, consistent with the main site's existing free-tier framing.
7. **FAQ reusing three existing FAQ items** (bank support, free plan contents, AI advisor cost) verbatim from klarmoney-landing.html so the two pages never contradict each other.

## 6. Timeline

Given 14 Aug 2026 has already passed and competitors are already ranking, this is a **catch-up sprint, not a future launch**:

- **Week 1 (now):** Build and publish the `/moneyhub-alternative` page. Draft the Reddit post. Identify the 4 outlets for outreach (4c) and draft short pitches — hold for founder review/send approval.
- **Week 2:** Publish Reddit post (r/UKPersonalFinance first, gauge response before r/FIREUK). Send outreach emails to the 4 roundup authors.
- **Weeks 3–4:** Monitor which channel drives signups (basic UTM tagging on the landing page link is enough — no paid analytics needed). Double down on whichever of Reddit/earned-placement performs.
- **After week 4:** Re-evaluate. The addressable Moneyhub-leaver pool is finite and shrinking as people settle on Emma/Snoop/WPS — if signups are flat by week 4, deprioritize and fold the `/moneyhub-alternative` page into evergreen "budgeting app alternative" SEO rather than running it as an active campaign.

**Reality check:** because the actual departure event already happened two weeks ago, this campaign is working against a cooling, not a peaking, wave of interest. Move this week or the opportunity mostly closes itself out.
