## 1. Important framing difference from the UK pitch — read before adapting further

The UK materials lead with the Cleo/Moneyhub competitive window and SEIS/EIS tax relief — both are UK-specific and **do not transfer to South Africa**:

- **No SA equivalent of SEIS/EIS exists for this raise.** Section 12J, the tax incentive that historically made SA venture investments attractive to local investors, has sunset with no announced successor as of this research (see klar-sa-funding-landscape.md, Section 0.1). SA investors in this raise get no special income tax relief. This should be stated plainly rather than hinted at — SA investors and fund managers will know the 12J landscape and will notice if it's glossed over.
- **The UK competitive narrative (Cleo's US retreat, Moneyhub's August 2026 closure) is a UK-market argument.** It should stay in the deck as evidence Klar is a credible, differentiated product with a live UK case study — but it is not, by itself, a reason for an SA investor to fund the SA opportunity. SA investors will want to know why *South Africa* is a good market for Klar, independent of what's happening in the UK.

We are early — pre-revenue, in soft launch — but the product is live, functional, and already serving South African users today at klarmoney.netlify.app.

## 5. Regulatory framing — a gap the UK docs don't have an SA answer for

The UK materials are explicit that Klar's core product requires no FCA registration to operate today, and that FCA AISP registration is a *future* milestone tied to Open Banking connectivity. **The existing docs do not address whether an SA regulatory equivalent applies or is planned.** South Africa does not have a single direct FCA-AISP equivalent, but depending on future functionality (e.g. any SA payments, account-aggregation, or advice-adjacent features), the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA) and/or National Credit Regulator could become relevant. **[FOUNDER INPUT NEEDED / FOLLOW-UP RESEARCH: this draft does not attempt to determine SA regulatory obligations, since this is a compliance question requiring proper legal input, not something to infer from general research. Flagging so it isn't silently omitted from an SA pitch that a fund's legal/compliance reviewer will ask about.]**
