## 1. Seedrs Campaign Description (About This Business)

**Your money, finally working with you — not against you.**

Most people in the UK have no clear picture of where their money goes. They're juggling two or three bank accounts, paying for subscriptions they forgot about, carrying debt with no clear payoff date, and making financial decisions based on gut feeling rather than real data. Existing apps either show you the data without helping you act on it, or charge US-dollar prices for a US-built product that doesn't quite fit UK financial life.

Klar is a UK personal finance app that combines transaction tracking, budget management, savings goals, debt payoff planning, and subscription management — with a live AI financial advisor built in. Ask it whether you can afford a holiday, whether to overpay your mortgage, or simply where your money went last month. It answers in plain English, in real time.

We've built Klar because the market timing is genuinely exceptional right now. Cleo — the AI-first money app — retreated to the United States and is only in a staged waitlist relaunch in the UK as of early 2026. Moneyhub, a respected consumer budgeting app, is permanently closing its consumer product in August 2026, leaving a pool of financially engaged, paying users looking for a replacement. No UK-headquartered competitor has a live conversational AI advisor today. That gap is ours.

Klar is live at klarmoney.netlify.app, available in UK (GBP) and South Africa (ZAR), with tiered pricing from £3.99/month. We're pre-revenue, in soft launch phase, and using this raise to fund growth, Open Banking integration, and our FCA AISP registration process.

**The ask:** We're raising £200,000 on Seedrs at a £1.5M pre-money valuation. Funds will be used to accelerate user acquisition, build out Open Banking connectivity, and progress FCA authorisation.

**Tax relief:** This round is expected to qualify for SEIS and EIS tax relief — meaning UK investors may receive up to 50% income tax relief on their investment. Advance assurance has been applied for from HMRC.

This is early-stage investing. We're honest about that. But the product is built, the market gap is real, and the window is open.
